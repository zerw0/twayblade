# Composer ＋ menu and polls (#181)

## Built

- The composer has a **＋** button (More message types) left of Attach.
  Its menu has **Poll**; Sticker and Voice Message follow in their own
  tasks (#183, #184) and are not shown until they work.
- **Create Poll** dialog: question, two to twenty answers (Add Answer,
  remove beyond two), and **Show results before the poll ends** (on by
  default; MSC3381 disclosed). Create Poll stays disabled until there is a
  question and two answers.
- **Poll card** in the timeline (all chat styles): question, one row per
  answer with a radio mark, and a bar per answer.
  - Open poll: click an answer to vote or change your vote. Disclosed polls
    show counts once you have voted ("Vote to see the results" before);
    undisclosed ones only when ended.
  - Ended poll: final counts, winners in bold with an accent bar; answers
    are plain rows, not buttons.
  - Your own open poll has **End Poll**, which asks first.
  - Screen readers hear the question, answers, your vote, counts when they
    are visible, and the status line.
- Core: `MessageKind::Poll` with `MessageRow::poll` from the SDK's
  aggregated `PollState`; `Conversation::send_poll`, `vote_poll`,
  `end_poll`; `PollDraft::validate`; `ConversationError::InvalidPoll`.
  Sent as MSC3381 unstable events (as Element does) with a plain-text
  fallback of the question and numbered answers.
- Previews: `--preview-poll`, `--preview-create-poll`.

## Verified

- Compile: strict workspace Clippy. Offline tests: draft validation and
  fallback (core); result visibility, status line and spoken text; a UI
  test for the cards (End Poll only on your open poll, final counts as
  rows) and the dialog (two answers by default, add and remove).
- Local Synapse (default `test` profile): in an encrypted room Alice sends a
  poll, Bob sees it and votes, Alice sees one vote, ends the poll, Bob sees
  the winner and a late vote is refused.
- Offline captures of the cards and the dialog reviewed.

## Not verified

- Element interoperability by hand; a live account; multiple-choice polls
  (only single choice is created; others display and accept one answer).
