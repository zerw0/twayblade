# Stickers (#183)

## Built

- Composer ＋ › **Sticker** opens a picker: one section per pack with a
  grid of pictures; choosing one sends it to the room the picker was opened
  for and closes the picker. Without packs it says where packs come from.
- Packs (MSC2545, as Element and other clients store them): your own pack
  (`im.ponies.user_emotes` account data), packs of rooms you enabled
  (`im.ponies.emote_rooms`), and the open room's packs
  (`im.ponies.room_emotes`). Only images usable as stickers are listed
  (image usage, else pack usage, else both). At most 50 packs and 500
  stickers per pack. Twayblade does not edit packs.
- Sent as `m.sticker` with the image's size and type. Incoming stickers
  (encrypted too) render as a 160 pt picture without a bubble, loaded like
  image previews; screen readers hear "Sticker, …".
- Core: `stickers` module (`Account::sticker_packs`,
  `Conversation::send_sticker`), `MessageKind::Sticker`, sticker media
  sources, `Account::sticker_thumbnail` (fitted, not cropped).
- Preview: `--preview-stickers`.

## Verified

- Compile: strict workspace Clippy. Offline tests: pack parsing (usage
  rules, invalid URLs, names) and enabled-room parsing; a UI test for the
  picker and a sticker row.
- Local Synapse (default `test` profile): Alice saves a pack in account
  data, the pack is listed, she sends the sticker into the encrypted room,
  Bob sees a sticker and its picture loads.
- Offline capture of the picker reviewed (pictures are placeholders offline).

## Not verified

- Packs made in Element or Cinny by hand; a live account; animated
  stickers (the first frame shows).
